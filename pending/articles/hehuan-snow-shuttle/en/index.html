How do you get to Hehuanshan to see the snow?

Quick answerWithout a car there are two ways to reach the snow on Hehuanshan (合歡山): Nantou Bus 6658A from Qingjing Farm to Wuling, Songxue Lodge and Xiaofengkou (booked online), or a charter from Taipei or Taichung. Nantou Bus says 6658A may stop running in thick fog, snow or ice, and in snow Highway 14A may be open only to cars fitted with snow chains, with precautionary night closures when the road freezes. Keep your plans flexible and check the Highway Bureau before you go.

Is there a shuttle bus up Hehuanshan in the snow season?

The public bus up Hehuanshan is Nantou Bus 6658A. It starts at the Qingjing Farm Guest House bus stop and calls at Wuling and Songxue Lodge, ending at Xiaofengkou. It is a Nantou Bus service, not Go Hike.

  • Times: on the current Nantou Bus timetable, three runs a day, leaving the Guest House at 08:20, 11:10 and 14:30, and returning from Xiaofengkou at 09:40, 12:30 and 15:50.
  • Fares: full fare NT$78 to Wuling, NT$83 to Songxue Lodge, NT$95 to Xiaofengkou (fare table from July 2019). Pay the driver (no change given) or use an EasyCard or iPASS.
  • Booking: online only. Booking opens 7 days ahead and closes at 12:00 the day before (earlier before public holidays); up to 4 seats per booking. Without a booking you can queue for empty seats.
  • Weather: Nantou Bus says the route may be suspended for safety in thick fog, heavy rain, snow or ice, which are exactly the days people go to see snow.

To reach Qingjing, Nantou Bus runs from Taichung Gancheng Station and Taichung HSR Station to Puli, where you change for Qingjing Farm. The first 6658A reaches Wuling at about 9:00, too late for sunrise.

How is the road controlled when it snows?

The road over Hehuanshan is Highway 14A. When snow and ice set in, the usual controls are:

  • Motorcycles, large buses and trucks over 3.5 tonnes are banned, and some sections are open only to cars fitted with snow chains.
  • On nights when the road freezes it may be closed as a precaution from 17:00 to 07:00, with a decision in the morning on chains-only or open.
  • From November 2026, Highway 14A from Cuifeng to Dayuling has a high-occupancy control for cars 00:00–08:00 every Saturday and Sunday, and on holidays of 3 days or more. Follow the notice and the officers on site.
  • The 2026 snow season ran from January 1 to March 1. Dates and controls are set by the Highway Bureau each year.

Snow and closures change daily. Check the Highway Bureau and the weather forecast before you set off, and leave a spare day if you can.

Where do you get off to see the snow?

The bus and charters use the same stops: Wuling, Songxue Lodge (next to Hehuan Mountain House) and Xiaofengkou. The Hehuan Main Peak trailhead is at about 29.5K on Highway 14A with no parking beside it, so you park at Wuling and walk about 10 minutes. The East Peak and Shimen Mountain start near Songxue Lodge; the North Peak trailhead is about 600 m from Xiaofengkou.

The Wuling, Kunyang and Hehuan Mountain House car parks charge for parking (2024 notice: cars NT$50 an hour on weekdays, NT$60 on holidays, up to NT$300 a day). Illegal parking at Wuling is often ticketed.

FAQ

Is there a shuttle bus to see the snow on Hehuanshan?

Yes. Nantou Bus 6658A runs from the Qingjing Farm Guest House to Wuling, Songxue Lodge and Xiaofengkou three times a day and must be booked online. It may stop in fog, snow or ice: check the Nantou Bus notice (Chinese) before you go.

Can a charter still get up Hehuanshan when it snows?

It depends on the controls that day. In snow and ice Highway 14A may be open only to cars with snow chains, and it may close overnight. We check the road before the trip; if there are controls, we talk with you about another drop-off point or another date.

Do you need a permit to see the snow on Hehuanshan?

Not for the Main, East, North and West Peaks or Shimen Mountain at present. Camping in the Hehuanshan area needs a separate application to Taroko National Park.

How long does it take from Taipei to Hehuanshan?

About 4 hours by charter from Taipei. By public transport you go via Taichung, Puli and Qingjing Farm, then take 6658A; the first bus reaches Wuling at about 9:00.
