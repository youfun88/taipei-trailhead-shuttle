How much does wedding photo shoot car hire cost?

Quick answerWedding car hire (婚紗包車) means hiring a van and driver to take the couple, photographer and styling team between shoot locations. Go Hike uses a 9-seat van from Taipei: the base fee covers 4 hours, from NT$2,500 in Taipei, then NT$500 an hour or NT$300 a half hour, with a changing tent on board. Entry tickets and parking are paid at cost. Below are worked examples using the published rates and what to send when you book.

Ask about wedding car hire

What does wedding car hire include?

  • A 9-seat van and driver for the couple, photographer and styling team, to the places you choose.
  • A changing tent on board, so you don't have to look for somewhere to change.
  • Room for gowns, camera gear and light stands; tell us about extra passengers, bicycles or large props when you book and we'll check the space.
  • Not included: entry tickets and parking, paid at cost.

Couples shooting on their own and wedding studios can both book; you choose the locations.

How is overtime charged? Three examples

The 4 hours start at the booked departure time, and up to 30 minutes of waiting is free. For example, if you book a 10:00 departure from your chosen address, the clock starts at 10:00 and the 30 minutes of waiting are not charged.

After the base fee, more than 10 minutes counts as half an hour (NT$300) and more than 30 minutes as an hour (NT$500). So:

  • 4 hours 20 minutes in Taipei: NT$2,500 + half an hour NT$300 = NT$2,800.
  • 5 hours in Taipei: NT$2,500 + one hour NT$500 = NT$3,000.
  • 4 hours 40 minutes in Yilan: NT$3,500 + one hour NT$500 = NT$4,000.

These use the published rates; tickets and parking are extra, and the quote confirmed when you book is what applies.

What should you send when you book?

  • The shoot date and rough times.
  • The area and the places you want to go.
  • How many people (couple, photographer, stylist, family).
  • Props: mention large props or bicycles; pets travel in a carrier, otherwise a cleaning fee applies.
