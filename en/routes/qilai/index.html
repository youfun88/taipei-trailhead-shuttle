Good to know
From 2026 the trail starts at Yuntian Temple (雲天宮). The old Tunyuan (屯原) section, 0 to 3K, is closed by landslides. The road to Yuntian Temple is rough, so check that the Nenggao Trail (能高越嶺) is open. Plan two days or more.

Qilai South Peak & Nanhua: trailhead access and permits

Public information checked in October 2026. Road controls, shuttles and permit rules change often, so check the official notices before you go.

Drop-off point
From January 1, 2026, the west Nenggao Trail starts at the Yuntian Temple trailhead (雲天宮登山口) and joins the old trail at about 3K. The Tunyuan section from 0 to 3K has been closed by landslides since April 2023. The Tunyuan service station has moved to Yuntian Temple, and every hiker must check in there.
Roads
The road to Yuntian Temple turns off Highway 14 at about 99K. It is bumpy and in poor shape; the Forestry Agency recommends a high-clearance vehicle or a shuttle. Park only in the designated lot, as large farm trucks use the road. Official drive times: Puli to Wushe 40 minutes, Wushe to Lushan 45 minutes, Lushan to the trailhead 30 minutes.
After drop-off
From the old Tunyuan start, Tianchi Lodge (天池山莊) was about 13 km. Starting from Yuntian Temple adds about 350 m of climbing and 1.5 to 2 hours. Tianchi Lodge currently has no meals and no gear rental.
Permits
Tianchi Lodge beds and tent sites open 60 days before and close 5 days before your stay, with a lottery 30 days before at 3 pm. Qilai South Peak is inside Taroko National Park and needs a park entry permit; Nanhua Mountain does not. No police mountain permit has been needed since 2020.
Weather
The trail and Tianchi Lodge close during typhoon warnings and heavy rain warnings.

Questions

Do we need to check the road to the Qilai South Peak trailhead first?

Yes. The Nenggao Trail (能高越嶺) and the road to the trailhead sometimes close because of conditions. We check how far we can reach before the trip.

Which trailhead is used for Qilai South Peak and Nanhua now?

From January 1, 2026, the trail starts at Yuntian Temple (雲天宮). The old Tunyuan section from 0 to 3K is closed by landslides, and the Tunyuan service station has moved to Yuntian Temple. The new start adds about 350 m of climbing and 1.5 to 2 hours.

Can a car reach the Yuntian Temple trailhead?

Official information says small cars can get there. But the road from Highway 14 at about 99K is bumpy and in poor shape, the Forestry Agency recommends a high-clearance vehicle or a shuttle, and you must park in the designated lot. Check the latest road conditions before you go.

What permits do I need for Qilai South Peak and Nanhua?

Book Tianchi Lodge beds or tent sites from 60 to 5 days before your stay; the lottery is 30 days before. Qilai South Peak is inside Taroko National Park and needs a park entry permit. Nanhua Mountain does not. No police mountain permit has been needed since 2020.

Round trip is two drives, there and back; same-day return means we drop you off and pick you up the same day.

What is the difference between one way, round trip and same-day return?

One way: from the city to the trailhead, or from the trailhead back to the city. Round trip: two drives, there and back, on the dates we agree, written on your trip confirmation. Same-day return: we drop you at the trailhead and pick you up at the agreed time the same day.

Will the driver wait if we come down late?

Before you start hiking, we confirm the pick-up time and place again, and the driver waits at the agreed time. We know hikes can run long because of fitness, weather or trail conditions. Please update us on your expected finish time during the hike if you can. Waiting time, any extra fees and emergency contacts are agreed when you book.

Can the 9-seat van really take 8 passengers and 8 big packs?

The 9-seat van takes up to 8 passengers, but luggage space depends on the size and number of packs and the van's layout. If everyone has a large hiking pack, tell us the pack sizes and count when you ask for a quote, and we help choose the right vehicle and how to carry everything.

Do I need to arrange park and mountain permits and cabins myself?

Yes. We provide the transport. Park and mountain permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are arranged by you. We can share where to apply for permits and cabins when you ask for a quote.

What if I need to cancel or change dates?

Tell us at least three days before departure for a full refund or a new date. If the mountain or road is closed by the authorities, you can cancel up to one day before.
