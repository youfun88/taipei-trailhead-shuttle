Luoma traverse: trailhead access and permits

Public information checked in October 2026. Road controls, shuttles and permit rules change often, so check the official notices before you go.

Start
The old Siyuan police post (思源埡口舊派出所) at about 45.6K on Highway 7A, Datong, Yilan. Turn left at the Forest Road 720 gate and cross the Luoyewei River to the trailhead.
Finish
Mashi Bridge (馬鰣橋) at about 50.6K on Highway 7A, Heping, Taichung, near Shengguang. The two ends are about 5 km apart by road.
Route
The usual direction is Siyuan Pass, Luoyewei Mountain, the Luoma giant tree, Mawuzuoyejun Mountain, then Mashi Bridge. About 10 to 12 km, 8 to 9 hours.
Watch out
Both ends have river crossings, and water rises after rain. There is no water on the way. The firebreak on Mawuzuoyejun Mountain is a steep slope, dangerous when wet or icy. Roadside parking on Highway 7A is not allowed.
Public transport
Kuo-Kuang buses 1751 and 1764 reach Siyuan, but the last bus back leaves around 2:30 pm, too early for most day traversers.
Permits
Sources disagree on whether the Yilan side needs a police mountain permit. Apply before you go, or check with the police.

Questions

The traverse starts and ends in different places. How does the shuttle work?

We drop you at the start and pick you up at the end, so you don't need to go back for a car. Tell us your direction when you ask for a quote; different entry and exit points affect the vehicle plan and the price.

Where does the Luoma traverse start and finish?

Usually you start at the old Siyuan police post (about 45.6K on Highway 7A) and finish at Mashi Bridge (about 50.6K, near Shengguang). The two ends are about 5 km apart by road. The traverse is about 10 to 12 km and takes 8 to 9 hours.

Can I do the Luoma traverse by bus?

Kuo-Kuang buses 1751 and 1764 reach Siyuan, but the last bus back leaves around 2:30 pm. Most day traversers finish later than that, so you need other transport.

Round trip is two drives, there and back; same-day return means we drop you off and pick you up the same day.

What is the difference between one way, round trip and same-day return?

One way: from the city to the trailhead, or from the trailhead back to the city. Round trip: two drives, there and back, on the dates we agree, written on your trip confirmation. Same-day return: we drop you at the trailhead and pick you up at the agreed time the same day.

Will the driver wait if we come down late?

Before you start hiking, we confirm the pick-up time and place again, and the driver waits at the agreed time. We know hikes can run long because of fitness, weather or trail conditions. Please update us on your expected finish time during the hike if you can. Waiting time, any extra fees and emergency contacts are agreed when you book.

Can the 9-seat van really take 8 passengers and 8 big packs?

The 9-seat van takes up to 8 passengers, but luggage space depends on the size and number of packs and the van's layout. If everyone has a large hiking pack, tell us the pack sizes and count when you ask for a quote, and we help choose the right vehicle and how to carry everything.

Do I need to arrange park and mountain permits and cabins myself?

Yes. We provide the transport. Park and mountain permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are arranged by you. We can share where to apply for permits and cabins when you ask for a quote.

What if I need to cancel or change dates?

Tell us at least three days before departure for a full refund or a new date. If the mountain or road is closed by the authorities, you can cancel up to one day before.
