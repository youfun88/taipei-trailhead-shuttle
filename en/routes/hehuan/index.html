Quick answers

How do I get from Taipei to Hehuanshan without a car?

There are two ways: a private shuttle, or high-speed rail plus buses.

Private shuttle: Go Hike (山行接駁) picks you up at Taipei Main Station or your address and drives you to the parking point for the peak you are hiking, about 4 hours from Taipei, then picks you up after the hike. You choose the pick-up time.

Public transport: take High Speed Rail to Taichung. From HSR Taichung Station (or Taichung Gancheng Station), Nantou Bus runs connecting services to Puli and on to Cingjing Farm (清境農場), changing route at Puli. At the Cingjing Farm Guest House stop (國民賓館), change to Nantou Bus 6658A to Wuling Pass (武嶺), Songxue Lodge (松雪樓) and Xiaofengkou (小風口).

Official source: Nantou Bus: Cingjing Farm to Wuling Pass and Hehuanshan (6658A) timetable and booking (Chinese), Nantou Bus: Taichung to Puli to Cingjing Farm connection (Chinese)

What are the limits of the Hehuanshan bus?

Under the current Nantou Bus timetable, 6658A runs three times a day. The first bus reaches Wuling Pass a little after 9 am and the last bus back leaves Xiaofengkou at 15:50, so it does not work for sunrise or a long day hike.

Seats are booked online, from 7 days before until noon the day before, up to 4 people per booking; without a booking you queue on the day. It is a mid-size bus with no luggage space, and it may not run in thick fog, heavy rain, snow or ice. Check the current timetable before you go.

A private shuttle runs on your schedule and stops where your hike starts. It is priced per vehicle, not per seat.

Official source: Nantou Bus: Cingjing Farm to Wuling Pass and Hehuanshan (6658A) timetable and booking (Chinese), Nantou Bus: Taichung to Puli to Cingjing Farm connection (Chinese)

How do I get to Hehuanshan from Taichung?

Hehuanshan is on Highway 14A in Ren-ai, Nantou. Where you get off depends on the peak: Wuling Pass or Kunyang for the Main Peak, Xiaofengkou for North Peak, and Songxue Lodge or Hehuan Villa for East Peak and Shimen Mountain.

By private shuttle, Go Hike (山行接駁) picks you up at Taichung HSR Station or your address and drives you to that point. By bus, take Nantou Bus from Taichung via Puli to Cingjing Farm, then 6658A, as described above.

Official source: Nantou Bus: Cingjing Farm to Wuling Pass and Hehuanshan (6658A) timetable and booking (Chinese), Nantou Bus: Taichung to Puli to Cingjing Farm connection (Chinese)

Hehuanshan peaks: trailhead access and permits

Public information checked in October 2026. Road controls, shuttles and permit rules change often, so check the official notices before you go.

Drop-off point
It depends on the peak. The Hehuan Main Peak trailhead is at about 29.5K on Highway 14A (台14甲) with no parking, so cars stop at Wuling Pass (武嶺, about 10 minutes on foot) or Kunyang (昆陽). East Peak and Shimen Mountain start near Songxue Lodge (松雪樓) and Hehuan Villa (合歡山莊). The North Peak trailhead is at about 36.7K; cars park at Xiaofengkou (小風口), about 600 m away.
Parking
The Wuling Pass, Kunyang and Hehuan Villa lots charge a fee (2024 rates: NT$50 an hour on weekdays, NT$60 on weekends, up to NT$300 a day for cars). Illegal parking at Wuling Pass is often ticketed.
Permits
Hehuan Main, East, North and West peaks and Shimen Mountain currently need no park or mountain permit. Since 2020 the area is outside the protected zone and Ren-ai Township is no longer a restricted mountain area. Camping in the Hehuan area needs separate approval from Taroko National Park.
Road rules
From October 24, 2026, on every holiday of 3 or more days, cars on Highway 14A between Cuifeng (翠峰) and Dayuling (大禹嶺) need at least 3 people from 00:00 to 08:00. Spring rhododendron season weekends have a similar rule.
Snow season
When there is snow or ice, buses, trucks over 3.5 tonnes and all motorcycles are banned, and some stretches are open only to cars with snow chains. On icy nights the road may close from 17:00 to 07:00 as a precaution. The 2026 snow season ran January 1 to March 1.
Lodging
Songxue Lodge is run by the Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency and booked through Taiwan Forest Recreation (台灣山林悠遊網).

Questions

What happens if there is thick fog or a road closure at Hehuanshan?

The high road at Hehuanshan often gets thick fog and cold, and may close in winter. We check road conditions before the trip. If the road is closed, we talk with you about another meeting point or a new date.

Where does the car stop for Hehuan Main Peak?

The Hehuan Main Peak trailhead is at about 29.5K on Highway 14A, with no parking next to it. Cars usually stop at Wuling Pass (about 10 minutes on foot) or Kunyang. For North Peak, walk about 600 m from Xiaofengkou. East Peak and Shimen Mountain start near Songxue Lodge and Hehuan Villa.

Do I need a permit to hike Hehuanshan?

Not for Hehuan Main, East, North and West peaks or Shimen Mountain. Since 2020 the area is outside the protected zone and Ren-ai Township is no longer a restricted mountain area. Only camping needs separate approval from Taroko National Park.

Is there a carpool rule on Hehuanshan on long weekends?

Yes. From October 24, 2026, on every holiday of 3 or more days, cars between Cuifeng and Dayuling on Highway 14A need at least 3 people from 00:00 to 08:00. Spring rhododendron season weekends have a similar rule. Check the Highway Bureau before you go.

What should I know about Hehuanshan in winter?

When there is snow or ice, motorcycles, buses and trucks over 3.5 tonnes are banned, and some stretches are open only to cars with snow chains. The road may also close on icy nights. Check the Highway Bureau road status before you go and keep your plan flexible.
