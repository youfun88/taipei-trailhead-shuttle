Dabajian: trailhead access and permits

Public information checked in October 2026. Road controls, shuttles and permit rules change often, so check the official notices before you go.

Drop-off point
Guanwu (觀霧, Guanwu Forest Recreation Area). Dalu Forest Road East (大鹿林道東線) has a checkpoint and gate at 0.4K. Regular vehicles cannot enter; only approved work vehicles can.
After drop-off
From Guanwu, walk about 19 km on Dalu Forest Road East to the Madala River trailhead (馬達拉溪登山口), about 7 hours. Then climb about 4 km to Jiujiu Cabin (九九山莊).
Roads
Freeway 3 Zhulin exit, Zhudong, County Road 122, Qingquan, Tuchang, then follow signs to Guanwu. Guanwu and the forest road often close after typhoons and heavy rain.
Permits
You need a Shei-Pa National Park entry permit, from 2 months to 5 days before entry. Climbing the Dabajian summit itself is banned; hikes go to the base of the peak (霸基).
Cabin
Jiujiu Cabin is run by the Forestry Agency Hsinchu branch. It is under renovation from March 30, 2026 to the end of 2028, with only 70 beds a day during the work.

Questions

Can the car drive into Dalulin Road?

We use the legal drop-off points at Guanwu (觀霧) and on Dalulin Road (大鹿林道). The hike may include a long walk on the forest road, so include that walking time when you set your pick-up time.

Can the car drive into Dalu Forest Road for Dabajian?

No. Dalu Forest Road East has a checkpoint at 0.4K and regular vehicles cannot enter. Cars stop at Guanwu. From there it is about 19 km on foot to the Madala River trailhead (about 7 hours), then about 4 km up to Jiujiu Cabin.

What permits do I need for Dabajian?

You need a Shei-Pa National Park entry permit (from 2 months to 5 days before entry). Jiujiu Cabin beds are booked separately through the Forestry Agency Hsinchu branch. The cabin is under renovation until the end of 2028, with only 70 beds a day. Climbing the Dabajian summit is banned.

How early do I need to leave Taipei for Dabajian?

Taipei to Guanwu is about 3.5 hours. Day one includes about 7 hours on the forest road before the climb to Jiujiu Cabin, so you usually start walking from Guanwu early in the morning.

What is the difference between one way, round trip and same-day return?

One way: from the city to the trailhead, or from the trailhead back to the city. Round trip: two drives, there and back, on the dates we agree, written on your trip confirmation. Same-day return: we drop you at the trailhead and pick you up at the agreed time the same day.

Will the driver wait if we come down late?

Before you start hiking, we confirm the pick-up time and place again, and the driver waits at the agreed time. We know hikes can run long because of fitness, weather or trail conditions. Please update us on your expected finish time during the hike if you can. Waiting time, any extra fees and emergency contacts are agreed when you book.

Can the 9-seat van really take 8 passengers and 8 big packs?

The 9-seat van takes up to 8 passengers, but luggage space depends on the size and number of packs and the van's layout. If everyone has a large hiking pack, tell us the pack sizes and count when you ask for a quote, and we help choose the right vehicle and how to carry everything.

Do I need to arrange park and mountain permits and cabins myself?

Yes. We provide the transport. Park and mountain permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are arranged by you. We can share where to apply for permits and cabins when you ask for a quote.

What if I need to cancel or change dates?

Tell us at least three days before departure for a full refund or a new date. If the mountain or road is closed by the authorities, you can cancel up to one day before.
