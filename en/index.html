Go Hike · 山行接駁 中文

Private hiking shuttle to Taiwan's mountain trailheads

Go Hike (山行接駁) drives hikers from Taipei and Taichung to the trailheads of Yushan, Xueshan, Hehuanshan, Jiaming Lake and other peaks, then picks you up after the hike. You pay per vehicle, not per person: one way from NT$5,000.

How it works

  1. Send your route, dates, number of hikers, pick-up place and preferred language.
  2. We confirm the vehicle and send a fixed quote. Pay the deposit to hold the car.
  3. We drive you to the trailhead and pick you up at the agreed time and place.

Questions

What is Go Hike (山行接駁)?

Go Hike (山行接駁, gohike.tw) is a private hiking shuttle in Taiwan. We drive hikers from Taipei or Taichung to mountain trailheads such as Yushan, Xueshan, Hehuanshan, Dabajian, Nanhu and Jiaming Lake, and pick you up again after the hike.

How much does a hiking shuttle in Taiwan cost?

Prices are per vehicle, not per person. One way starts at NT$5,000. Each route's price is in the table on this page, for a 5-seat car (up to 4 passengers) or a 9-seat van (up to 8 passengers), from Taipei or Taichung.

What is the difference between one way, round trip and same-day return?

One way is a single drive, to the trailhead or back to the city. Round trip is two drives on the dates you choose, for hikes of two days or more. Same-day return means we drop you off and pick you up on the same day.

Do I need to arrange park and mountain permits myself?

Yes. We provide the transport. Park and mountain entry permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are arranged by you. We can share where to apply when you ask for a quote. Applications are made at hike.taiwan.gov.tw.

Can I book from Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia or elsewhere?

Yes. Send your route, dates, number of hikers, pick-up place and flight arrival date by WhatsApp, LINE or email, and tell us your preferred language. We confirm the car and reply with a quote.

How do overseas hikers pay?

The deposit can be paid by PayPal, bank transfer or LINE Pay. The balance is paid in New Taiwan dollars, in cash or by bank transfer. We confirm the payment method with your quote.

What if I need to cancel or change dates?

Tell us at least three days before departure for a full refund or a date change. If the mountain or road is closed by the authorities, you can cancel up to one day before.

Will the driver wait if we come down late?

Before you start hiking we confirm the pick-up time and place again, and the driver waits at the agreed time. Please update us on your expected finish time during the hike. Waiting time, any extra fees and emergency contacts are agreed when you book.

How long is the drive from Taipei to the trailheads?

It depends on the route: about 4 hours to Hehuanshan, 4.5 to 5 hours to Tataka for Yushan, and 7 to 8 hours to Xiangyang for Jiaming Lake. If you have just landed in Taiwan, allow time to rest before a long drive.
