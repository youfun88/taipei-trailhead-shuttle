FAQ

        
Before you go, you may want to know

        

          
          
          
        

        
        

          

            How do I book a shuttle?
            
Send us your route, start and return dates, number of passengers, pick-up place and time. We confirm the trip and vehicle, then send a quote. The booking is confirmed once we both agree the price and pick-up details and the agreed deposit is paid.

          

          

            Do I need to pay a deposit?
            
Yes. Once the vehicle and trip are confirmed, we send a formal quote with deposit details. The deposit amount and when and how to pay the balance are all agreed before you book. The car is held for you once the deposit is paid.

          

          

            How can I pay?
            
The deposit can be paid by bank transfer or LINE Pay, and overseas visitors can use PayPal. The balance is paid in New Taiwan dollars, in cash or by bank transfer. We send payment details and deadlines with your quote. If you are overseas, tell us where you will pay from and we confirm the best method.

          

          

            What if I need to cancel or change dates?
            
Tell us at least three days before departure for a full refund or a new date. If the mountain or road is closed by the authorities, you can cancel up to one day before.

          

          

            Is the website price the final price?
            
Website prices are reference prices for the whole vehicle, by starting city, vehicle and trip type. Your quote depends on the pick-up place, times, extra stops, number of days and road conditions. Any extra cost is listed before you book, and the trip confirmation lists anything not included, such as private trailhead, park entry or parking fees.

          

          

            What does the price include, and what do I arrange myself?
            
The price covers the whole vehicle for the drive from your pick-up place to the trailhead, or from the trailhead back to the city. The 5-seat car takes up to 4 passengers and the 9-seat van up to 8. Other costs, such as private trailhead, park entry or parking fees, are not in the website price; they are listed on your trip confirmation. Park and mountain permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are yours to arrange. We can share where to apply when you ask for a quote.

          

          

            How is the price worked out?
            
You pay per vehicle. We have a 5-seat car and a 9-seat van, starting from Taipei or Taichung. One way starts at NT$5,000, and each route's price is in the price table above. For trips with no price in the table, or trailheads not listed, send us your destination, date and group size and we reply with a clear price.

          

          

            Does the driver speak English?
            
Our driver speaks some basic English, enough for pick-up times and places. For details, message us in English on WhatsApp, LINE or email, and we confirm everything in writing before the trip.

          

        

        

          

            What is the difference between one way, round trip and same-day return?
            
One way: from the city to the trailhead, or from the trailhead back to the city. Round trip: two drives, there and back, on the dates we agree, written on your trip confirmation. Same-day return: we drop you at the trailhead and pick you up at the agreed time the same day.

          

          

            Will the driver wait if we come down late?
            
Before you start hiking, we confirm the pick-up time and place again, and the driver waits at the agreed time. We know hikes can run long because of fitness, weather or trail conditions. Please update us on your expected finish time during the hike if you can. Waiting time, any extra fees and emergency contacts are agreed when you book.

          

          

            Can I change the pick-up place or add a stop?
            
If you want to leave from a hotel, home or another address, tell us when you ask for a quote. We check distance, route and vehicle schedule, and tell you any extra cost in advance. Last-minute changes depend on that day's roads and schedule.

          

          

            What if the road to the trailhead is closed?
            
Mountain roads can close because of weather, roadworks, disasters or official restrictions. We check the latest road information and choose a legal, safe meeting point. If the car can't reach the original place, we discuss another meeting point, a different start time or a new date with you. If the trip can't go ahead at all, refunds follow the applicable rules and what we agreed.

          

          

            Can the 9-seat van really take 8 passengers and 8 big packs?
            
The 9-seat van takes up to 8 passengers, but luggage space depends on the size and number of packs and the van's layout. If everyone has a large hiking pack, tell us the pack sizes and count when you ask for a quote, and we help choose the right vehicle and how to carry everything.

          

          

            What vehicles do you have, and how many people fit?
            
Two types: the 5-seat car takes up to 4 passengers; the 9-seat van is a Volkswagen Caravelle or similar and takes up to 8 passengers. If you have a larger group or lots of big packs, tell us when you ask for a quote and we suggest a vehicle.

          

          

            Can you go to trailheads not listed on the website?
            
Just ask. The website lists our most common routes. For other trailheads, choose "Other trailhead" in the form and write the place in the notes.

          

          

            Do I need to arrange park and mountain permits and cabins myself?
            
Yes. We provide the transport. Park and mountain permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are arranged by you. We can share where to apply for permits and cabins when you ask for a quote.

          

        

        

          

            Can I book from Hong Kong, Malaysia or Singapore?
            
Yes. The form accepts international numbers such as +852, +60 and +65, or you can leave just an email. Choose how you want us to contact you (WhatsApp, LINE or email). Please include your flight arrival date and preferred language, and we reply with a quote after confirming the vehicle and trip.

          

          

            How do overseas visitors pay?
            
The deposit can be paid by PayPal, bank transfer or LINE Pay. The balance is paid in New Taiwan dollars, in cash or by bank transfer. Tell us where you will pay from when you ask for a quote, and we confirm the best method with your quote.

          

          

            How long is the drive from Taipei to the trailheads?
            
It depends on the route: about 4 hours to Hehuanshan, 4.5 to 5 hours to Tataka for Yushan, and 7 to 8 hours to Xiangyang for Jiaming Lake. If you have just landed in Taiwan, allow enough time to travel and rest. Each route card shows the drive time.

          

          

            Do overseas visitors also need their own park and mountain permits?
            
Yes. We provide the transport. Park and mountain permits, cabins or campsites, hiking insurance and gear are arranged by you. We can share where to apply when you ask for a quote. Applications are made at hike.taiwan.gov.tw.
